# Caustic soda (NaOH) and hydrochloric acid (HCl) prices: US Gulf Coast / Brownsville, Texas vs inland Minas Gerais, Brazil (2025–2026)

Research date: 4 November 2026. The most recent hard price point found is from August 2026, and most are from Q1–Q2 2026. No public assessment from September or October 2026 with a number was found.

Terms used below:
- **dmt** (dry metric tonne): price per tonne of 100% NaOH contained. One dmt of 50% liquid caustic is 2 wet tonnes of solution.
- **dst** (dry short ton): 2,000 lb of 100% NaOH. US domestic prices are quoted this way. Multiply $/dst by 1.1023 to get $/dmt.
- **FOB** (free on board): the price at the loading port, before freight.
- **CFR / CIF** (cost and freight / cost, insurance and freight): the price at the destination port, before import duty and inland freight.
- **DDP** (delivered duty paid): delivered to the buyer with import duties paid.
- **ECU** (electrochemical unit): 1 t chlorine plus about 1.1 t NaOH, the joint output of a chlor-alkali cell.
- **PEA** (Preliminary Economic Assessment): the Tiros July 2026 study whose prices are being checked here.

## 1. US Gulf caustic soda prices 2024–2026 (liquid 50%, solid/flake, basis conventions, delivered to South Texas)

### Takeaway
US Gulf export (FOB) liquid caustic traded between about **US$330 and US$490/dmt** from December 2025 to May 2026. It spiked in March–May 2026 (Middle East war and the Strait of Hormuz closure), then softened through mid-2026. Indexes put it around **US$400–410/t in July–August 2026**. US *domestic* prices sit well above export prices: spot was **US$475–500/dst (about US$524–551/dmt)** and the published contract index was **US$655–690/dst (about US$722–761/dmt)** in April–May 2026. A Brownsville buyer would pay a domestic-type price plus freight, not the export netback.

**Solid/flake:**

- Flakes carry a "40–60% premium over liquid lye on a per-unit NaOH basis" (evaporation energy, packaging, handling). Chemtradeasia gives no primary source for this — [Chemtradeasia](https://www.chemtradeasia.com/market-insights/caustic-soda-flakes-price-index-2026-supply)

### Inferences
- **Basis matters.** US domestic contract (US$/dst) and export (US$/dmt) are different markets. Contract indexes are list-type numbers, and large buyers typically negotiate discounts from them. The export price is the floor US producers accept for surplus. A Brownsville plant buying about 100 kt/y of NaOH (dry basis) would negotiate a term contract, probably somewhere between export netback and spot domestic. That implies **about US$450–550/dmt FOB Gulf plant** at mid-2026 market levels (estimate).
- **Freight to Brownsville.** Gulf Coast chlor-alkali plants (Freeport, Deer Park, Lake Charles, Corpus Christi area) can reach the Port of Brownsville by barge on the Gulf Intracoastal Waterway or by rail tank car. Freight for liquid 50% is paid on *wet* tonnes, so each US$1/wet t of freight costs about US$2/dmt. An unsourced planning estimate is **US$25–50/wet t (US$50–100/dmt)**. That gives a **Brownsville delivered estimate of US$500–650/dmt** for 50% liquid at 2026 price levels.
- Buying 50% liquid in Texas avoids the 40–60% flake premium. The process uses NaOH in solution (neutralisation and hydrolysis), so a Texas plant has no reason to buy solid.

### Gaps
- No public September–October 2026 US Gulf FOB or US contract number was found (Argus, ICIS and Platts are paywalled).
- No sourced barge or rail freight rate for caustic from Houston or Freeport to Brownsville was found.
- No US domestic flake price was found. US producers (OxyChem, for example) do make flakes and beads, but list prices are not public.

## 2. Brazil caustic soda: imports, CFR prices, domestic producers, inland delivered cost to Minas Gerais, anti-dumping status

### Takeaway
Brazil is a structural importer of liquid caustic, mostly from the US Gulf. It took about 50–60% of US caustic exports in 2024–early 2025, and Argus expected roughly **350,000 dmt/y more imports in 2026** after Braskem indefinitely idled its 410,000-ECU Maceió plant (about 25% of Brazilian chlor-alkali capacity). **Brazil does not currently apply an anti-dumping duty on US caustic soda.** A 2021–22 investigation into US liquid caustic was closed in August 2022 with no measure. The normal import tariff on liquid caustic (NCM 2815.12.00) is listed at 7.2%. Liquid CFR Brazil in Q1 2026 was cited at about **US$513/dmt**.

### Cited Findings
- Braskem has indefinitely idled its 410,000-ECU diaphragm plant in Maceió (about 25% of Brazil's chlor-alkali capacity). Argus expects Brazil to import about **350,000 dmt/y more caustic in 2026 than in 2025**, mainly from the US Gulf, and Latin American imports to hit a record in 2026 after a record 2025 driven by pulp and paper — [Argus Nov 2025](https://view.argusmedia.com/rs/584-BUW-606/images/CHE-Argus-Chlor-Alkali-Outlook-Report-Sample-Nov2025.pdf?version=0)
- Brazil took 50% of US caustic exports in 2024 (volume growing 12%/y) and over 60% in January 2025. Demand growth came from pulp (Suzano Cerrado, 2.55 Mt/y, started July 2024) — [S&P Global, Mar 2025](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/chemicals/032825-brazils-pulp-paper-industry-to-increase-demand-for-us-caustic-soda-plstico-brasil)
- Brazil imported 908,720 t of caustic soda January–August 2023. The US Gulf is the regional benchmark, supplying about 71.5% of South and Central American imports. Domestic and import suppliers price "at relatively close levels" — [ICIS, Nov 9, 2023](https://icis.com/explore/resources/news/2023/11/09/10942686/apla-23-brazil-caustic-soda-anticipates-potential-new-demand-through-upcoming-investments-in-mining)
- May 2026: Argus noted US Gulf producers gained a cost advantage into Brazil as the US premium over Northeast Asia narrowed. Earlier, lower Asian prices opened a transpacific arbitrage to Latin America — [Argus May 22, 2026](https://view.argusmedia.com/rs/584-BUW-606/images/CHE-Sample-report-Chlor-Alkali-20260522.pdf)
- Q1 2026: Brazil/LatAm liquid caustic CIF about **US$513/t (dry basis)**, described as "elevated and import-dependent" — [Chemtradeasia](https://www.chemtradeasia.com/market-insights/caustic-soda-flakes-price-index-2026-supply)
- Procurement Resource: Brazil CIF US$266.77/t (July 2026) and US$237.13/t (August 2026). The grade is not stated — [Procurement Resource](https://www.procurementresource.com/resource-center/caustic-soda-price-trends). **Caution:** these are well below US Gulf FOB in the same months (US$399–412). They are most likely quoted per *wet* tonne of 50% solution, which would be about US$474–534/dmt. Do not use them as dmt values.
- **Domestic producers:** Unipar Carbocloro has chlor-alkali plants at Cubatão and Santo André (São Paulo state) and Camaçari (Bahia), plus Bahía Blanca (Argentina). Brazil electrolysis utilisation was 84% in 2Q26. Cubatão's new membrane technology started in March 2026 — [Unipar 2Q26](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/3c0b3516-7dff-44a5-946f-20e7ec87dfa0/bd18e23a-20cf-5455-dc94-ecb4f607acce?origin=2); [Unipar 2Q25](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/3c0b3516-7dff-44a5-946f-20e7ec87dfa0/fcd2c57a-499e-4d2a-5114-96917e2ae6fa?origin=1). Braskem's Maceió plant is idled (above).
- **Anti-dumping on US caustic:** Circular SECEX nº 7 (17 February 2021) opened an anti-dumping investigation into *liquid* caustic soda (NCM 2815.12.00) from the USA. Circular SECEX nº 34 (5 August 2022, published in the official gazette (DOU) 9 August 2022) **closed it without applying any anti-dumping measure**. Authorities found the imports did not cause the domestic industry's injury — [Normas Brasil, Circular SECEX 34/2022](https://www.normasbrasil.com.br/norma/circular-34-2022_434958.html)
- In 2025 Brazil *did* raise anti-dumping on US **PVC** (not caustic) from 8.2% to 43.7% (GECEX Resolution 737, May 2025) — [Unipar 2Q25](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/3c0b3516-7dff-44a5-946f-20e7ec87dfa0/fcd2c57a-499e-4d2a-5114-96917e2ae6fa?origin=1)
- Brazil's import tariff (II, under the common external tariff, TEC) for NCM 2815.12.00 (liquid caustic) is listed at **7.2%** (Res. Gecex 272/2021) — [Tabelas Fiscais](https://tabelasfiscais.com.br/ncm/28151200)

### Inferences
- **Building a CFR-to-Tiros delivered price for 50% liquid** (estimate):
  - CFR Santos or Vitória about US$430–530/dmt (2026 range: US Gulf FOB US$330–490 plus ocean freight).
  - Plus 7.2% duty, about US$30–38/dmt.
  - Plus port handling and storage.
  - Plus road freight of about 700–900 km to São Gotardo/Tiros (Alto Paranaíba, Minas Gerais), paid on wet tonnes. That would be roughly US$35–60/wet t, or about US$70–120/dmt (unsourced planning estimate).
  - **Delivered liquid total: about US$550–700/dmt.**
- **For 98% solid (the PEA basis).** Chinese flake at US$445–460/t FOB, plus about US$40–70/t ocean freight, 7.2% duty (the 2815.11.00 rate is unconfirmed) and about US$35–60/t inland freight, gives **about US$560–640/t delivered**. Domestic Brazilian flake is likely similar or higher, given the reported 40–60% flake premium.
- **The PEA's US$420/t delivered for 98% NaOH (about US$429/dmt contained) looks low.** It is roughly equal to *FOB US Gulf* or *FOB China* levels with no freight, duty or flake premium. It would require a contract at the bottom of the cycle (for example, the December 2025 level of US$330/dmt) plus very cheap logistics. The likely 2026 delivered range is about **US$550–700/t**, which adds roughly **US$13–28 M/y** to the PEA's reported ~US$43 M/y.
- **Brownsville vs Tiros for NaOH:** Brownsville avoids ocean freight (~US$40–70/t wet), the 7.2% Brazilian duty, about 800 km of Brazilian road freight, and the solid premium (by buying 50% liquid). A rough saving of **US$50–150/dmt** is plausible, or about **US$5–15 M/y** on 100 kt/y. Two caveats:
  - US domestic prices carry a premium over export netback, which shrinks the saving.
  - The ore or concentrate would have to be shipped from Brazil to Texas instead, which is likely much larger tonnage than the reagent.

### Gaps
- No Comex Stat (Brazil trade statistics) import volume for 2025 or 2026 was retrieved, because the tables would not load. The ICIS 2023 figure (908,720 t for January–August 2023) is the latest hard volume found.
- No confirmation that no *new* anti-dumping case on US caustic was opened from 2023 to 2026. Searches found none.
- No Brazilian domestic list price (Unipar or Braskem, in R$/t) was found.
- No sourced Santos-to-Alto Paranaíba road freight rate was found. Argus reports fertilizer road freight rose up to 70% on some routes after the ANTT (Brazil's national land transport agency) minimum freight table was enforced from October 2025, but gives no absolute rates — [Argus](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2770686-viewpoint-brazil-freight-rates-rise-on-record-soy-crop)

## 3. HCl 32–36%: US Gulf and Brazil prices; long-distance shipping

### Takeaway
HCl is a cheap by-product (from chlorination, PVC and isocyanate chains), and prices fell through 2025. US quarterly averages ran from about **US$350/t (Q2 2025, delivered US Gulf Coast)** to about US$250/t (Q4 2025) and about US$180/t (Q1 2026). Brazil, at Cubatão delivered-duty-paid (the Unipar plant area), was about **US$410–430/t through 2025**, falling to about US$240/t in Q1 2026. The PEA's US$200/t delivered for 34% HCl is near the low end of the observed ranges for both countries. It is optimistic for an inland Minas Gerais site.

### Cited Findings
- ChemAnalyst HCl (concentration and basis partly unstated):
  - **USA:** Q2 2025 about US$350/t (delivered US Gulf Coast). Q3 2025 average about US$338/t. Q4 2025 average about US$251/t (supply abundant, imports from Canada and Germany). Q1 2026 average about US$181/t (Los Angeles delivered basis).
  - **Brazil:** Q2 2025 about US$431/t (delivered duty paid, Cubatão). Q3 2025 about US$415/t. Q4 2025 about US$409/t (imports from the US and Belgium). Q1 2026 about US$242/t.
  - Source: [ChemAnalyst HCl pricing page](https://www.chemanalyst.com/Pricing-data/hydrochloric-acid-61)
- December 2025: US HCl rose on tight year-end supply and scarce inventories. Steel pickling is a key demand driver — [ChemAnalyst, Jan 12, 2026](https://www.chemanalyst.com/NewsAndDeals/NewsDetails/hcl-prices-rise-across-major-markets-in-december-2025-40739)
- April–May 2026: HCl markets had "ample supply, soft prices" globally — [Chemtradeasia Apr 2026](https://www.chemtradeasia.com/market-insights/hydrochloric-acid-market-april-2026-ample-supply-soft-prices); [Chemtradeasia May 2026](https://www.chemtradeasia.com/market-insights/hydrochloric-acid-prices-softened-major-regions-may-2026)
- Unipar lists HCl as part of its Brazilian product portfolio but gives no volumes or prices — [Unipar 2Q25](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/3c0b3516-7dff-44a5-946f-20e7ec87dfa0/fcd2c57a-499e-4d2a-5114-96917e2ae6fa?origin=1)

### Inferences
- **The ChemAnalyst figures are inconsistent.** The Q1 2026 Brazil average of about US$242/t sits beside a reported +4% quarter-on-quarter change from about US$409/t, which does not reconcile. The US Q1 2026 figure has the same problem (~US$181 vs +3.6% from ~US$251). The basis probably changed between quarters (for example, between per tonne of solution and per tonne of 100% HCl, or between locations). Treat the absolute levels as ±30%.
- **Freight dominates HCl.** 32–36% HCl is about two-thirds water and is corrosive, needing rubber-lined tank cars or trucks. Its value per tonne is low (~US$100–400/t), so freight is a large share of delivered cost and long-haul shipping is uneconomic relative to product value. (This is general industry knowledge; no specific source was retrieved.) For Tiros, Cubatão (Unipar) to São Gotardo is roughly 800 km by road. Freight of US$40–70/t on top of an ex-works price of US$150–250/t implies **US$200–320/t delivered**, so US$200/t is at the low end.
- **Texas advantage for HCl.** Brownsville is near the large Gulf Coast by-product HCl pool. Delivered **US$150–300/t** for 32–36% HCl looks plausible (estimate), likely lower than inland Minas Gerais. However, 82 kt/y × US$50–100/t difference is only **about US$4–8 M/y**.
- The plant could also regenerate HCl (spray roasting / pyrohydrolysis) or make it on site from chlorine. That is out of scope here, but it changes how much HCl price matters.

### Gaps
- No Argus, ICIS or Platts HCl contract assessment for US Gulf or Brazil was found in public excerpts.
- No sourced concentration basis for the ChemAnalyst numbers.
- No sourced freight-distance economics for HCl.

## 4. Consistency check of PEA prices (NaOH US$420/t, HCl 34% US$200/t, H2SO4 96% US$170/t delivered Minas Gerais)

### Takeaway
- **NaOH at US$420/t (98% solid, delivered) is optimistic** by roughly 25–65% against 2026 markets.
- **HCl at US$200/t is at the low end** but defensible in the soft 2026 HCl market.
- **H2SO4 at US$170/t delivered inland was reasonable before the 2026 sulfur shock.** It is low against 2026 spot levels, when sulfur roughly doubled.

### Cited Findings
- **Sulfuric acid:**
  - CFR Chile (the regional acid benchmark) was about US$190/t in February 2026, about US$300 on April 8 and about US$380 on April 15, 2026, then corrected to about US$175–180/t by July 2026 — [Discovery Alert summarising Fastmarkets/Argus](https://discoveryalert.com/cfr-chile-sulfuric-acid-assessment-price-volatility-2026/). This is a secondary aggregator; verify against the primary assessment.
  - Sulfuric acid CFR US Gulf was US$400/t on May 6, 2026, up from US$155/t on February 25 (before the war). Elemental sulfur CFR Brazil was US$1,150/t on May 7, 2026, up from US$525/t on February 26 — [S&P Global, May 7, 2026](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/agriculture/050726-global-sulfur-prices-surge-on-middle-east-disruptions-china-export-ban)
- **Sulfur:**
  - Middle East FOB granular sulfur went from about US$170/t in early 2025 to US$520/t at year-end 2025. China CFR was US$505 on December 18, 2025 — [Argus, Jan 2, 2026](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2771376-precos-globais-do-enxofre-devem-permanecer-elevados)
  - QatarEnergy's sulfur price was US$740/t FOB (May 2026, a record) — [CRU/BCInsight, May 21, 2026](https://www.bcinsight.crugroup.com/2026/05/21/price-trends-75/). It was raised 9% to US$805/t FOB for June 2026 — [Fertilizer Daily](https://www.fertilizerdaily.com/20260602-qatarenergy-raises-june-sulphur-price-9-to-805t-fob/)
  - By late August 2026, most sulfur import markets were "stagnant" on weak demand — [CRU/BCInsight](https://www.bcinsight.crugroup.com/2026/01/28/price-trends-72/)
- NaOH and HCl evidence: see sections 1–3.

### Inferences
- **NaOH:** US$420/t for 98% solid is about US$429/dmt contained. That is at or below *FOB* Gulf and China levels before freight, duty and flake premium. A realistic 2026 delivered Tiros range is **US$550–700/t**. Sensitivity: every +US$100/t adds about +US$10 M/y.
- **H2SO4:**
  - If the PEA plant burns its own sulfur, imported sulfur at ~US$500–1,150/t CFR Brazil (2026) would give acid well above US$170/t. Each tonne of acid needs about 0.33 t of sulfur.
  - If acid is bought from Minas Gerais phosphate-complex producers, US$170/t matches CFR Chile at July 2026 (~US$175–180) but not delivered inland after freight.
  - Suggested planning range: **US$170–250/t delivered**, with a high case of US$350+ under 2026 shock conditions.
  - The Brownsville comparison should use US Gulf acid (US$155/t in February 2026 and US$400/t in May 2026), with a note that 2026 prices are volatile.
- **HCl:** US$200/t is plausible in the soft 2026 market but is a low-case number for an inland site. Use **US$200–320/t** delivered for Tiros and **US$150–300/t** for Brownsville.

### Gaps
- No Brazil-specific sulfuric acid assessment (CFR Santos or delivered Minas Gerais) was found.
- No primary October 2026 acid price was found.
- The PEA's acid source (bought or self-produced) is not known from this research.

## 5. Minor reagents: magnesium hydroxide and soda ash, US vs Brazil

### Takeaway
Magnesium hydroxide is about **US$800/t in the US vs about US$1,030/t in Brazil/Latin America** (IMARC, Q3 2025, grade and basis unspecified). US soda ash is very cheap at about **US$183–185/t** (natural trona from Wyoming, Q3–Q4 2025). Brazil imports most of its soda ash (largely from the US), so Brazilian soda ash would carry ocean and inland freight on top.

### Cited Findings
- Magnesium hydroxide: USA US$796/t (Q3 2025, +2.1% quarter-on-quarter). Brazil/Latin America US$1,034/t (+1.9%), attributed to limited regional capacity and mining and construction demand. The grade (powder or slurry) and basis are not stated — [IMARC, Dec 26, 2025](https://www.imarcgroup.com/news/magnesium-hydroxide-price-index)
- Soda ash USA:
  - Q1 2025 flat at about US$180/t ex-works Wyoming.
  - Q3 2025 average about US$185/t; Q4 2025 about US$183/t.
  - Latin American export demand tightened Gulf Coast supply in Q4 2025.
  - Source: [ChemAnalyst soda ash](https://chemanalyst.com/Pricing-data/soda-ash-76)
- US soda ash prices were soft in early February 2026 and again in mid-May 2026 on global oversupply — [ChemAnalyst Feb 2026](https://www.chemanalyst.com/NewsAndDeals/NewsDetails/us-soda-ash-prices-down-0-55-on-muted-spot-demand-in-early-41180); [ChemAnalyst May 2026](https://www.chemanalyst.com/NewsAndDeals/NewsDetails/global-oversupply-keeps-us-soda-ash-prices-soft-in-mid-may-42502)

### Inferences
- Wyoming ex-works soda ash must still travel about 1,500+ miles by rail to Brownsville. Delivered Brownsville soda ash is plausibly **US$250–320/t** (estimate). Brazil delivered inland is plausibly **US$350–450/t** (estimate: CFR Brazil plus duty plus inland freight).
- Magnesium hydroxide is about 25–30% cheaper in the US on these index numbers. Gulf Coast seawater/brine-based magnesium hydroxide slurry is available, so Brownsville likely has an edge.

### Gaps
- No Brazil soda ash price was found.
- No grade or basis for the magnesium hydroxide indexes; slurry and powder prices differ greatly.
- IMARC and ChemAnalyst are secondary index publishers with partly paywalled methods, so treat their figures as indicative only.
